# GDPR Compliance in Ireland in 2026: The Best Providers for EU Regulation 2016/679 Compliance

In 2026, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is no longer just a legal obligation, but a **strategic element** for business reputation and competitiveness. Administrative sanctions, reputational risks, and increasingly frequent compliance requests from clients and stakeholders make it essential to **be compliant, always and concretely**.

The GDPR requires a systematic approach to personal data protection, which includes: treatment assessments, technical and organizational measures, roles (Controller, DPO, Processor), incident management, DPIA, treatment registry, and security by design.

In this guide you'll find the **10 best Irish providers for GDPR services in 2026**, with focus on consulting, audit, DPO support, training, and integrated technical measures.
